How it Works

What if you had just one weekend to make a film—start to finish?

The 48 Hour Film Project is a global filmmaking challenge where teams write, shoot, and edit a short film in only 48 hours. No time to overthink. Just pure creativity, collaboration, and a race against the clock.

Here’s how your weekend unfolds:

FRIDAY – Kickoff & Writing

Your team gets your genre, plus a character, prop, and line of dialogue that must be included in your film. Then it’s straight into brainstorming, writing, and prepping for your shoot.

SATURDAY – Filming

Lights, camera, action. You shoot your film while your editor can already start assembling footage. It’s a full day of creativity, problem-solving, teamwork, and making it happen.

SUNDAY – Editing & Delivery

Everything comes together. Editing, sound, music, finishing touches—and then the final sprint to upload your film before the deadline to the Dashboard online.

Next... you see it on the big screen

Your film premieres in a real cinema with an audience. One to two weeks after Drop Off, you, your friends, family, and fellow filmmakers watch what you created in just one weekend. Awards are given, and the winner of Best Film moves on to Filmapalooza, our annual international film festival.

It’s intense. It’s unpredictable. It’s one of the most fun things you’ll ever do as a filmmaker.

Ready to take on the challenge?

What Happens After the Local 48?

The top films earn more exposure. The Best Film in each city screens at our annual international film festival—Filmapalooza. This fest features screenings, workshops and parties and is attended by filmmakers from around the world. At the Awards Ceremony we announce the Grand Champion of the Year!

How Much is the Registration Fee?

The registration fee is for the entire team.

The fee varies depending on location. In US cities, the fees are the same. For 48s outside of the US, the fee differs.

In the United States:

First Year Cities: From $135 to $155 for each team

Repeat Cities: From $158 to $198 for each team

Please visit the webpage for that location.
